Privacy policy

BEATBOX TOOLS BY SAVIOR · UPDATED 9 NOVEMBER 2026

Short version: everything you make in this app stays on your own device. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. I can't see your recordings or your data.

Who I am

Beatbox Tools is a free app made by Xavier Chua, a solo developer who beatboxes as Savior. In this policy, "I" and "the app" mean me and this app.

What the app stores

The app saves the following on your device only, in your browser or installed app storage:

  • Voice notes you record, and recordings you save from the loop pad
  • Your routines, practice log, ladder records, pattern grid and tool settings
  • Your choices in Settings, such as theme and accent colour

None of this is sent to me or to any server. If you clear the app's data or uninstall it, it is gone, unless you made a backup file yourself.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access so you can record voice notes, use the loop pad, and use the tuner and spectrum tools. Audio is processed on your device while you use these tools. It is never uploaded. The microphone is only on while you are recording or using one of these tools, and it is released when you stop or leave the tool. You can turn the permission off at any time in your phone or browser settings.

Backups and sharing

The "Back up everything" button creates a file on your device. Where it goes after that is up to you. If you use Share or Download on a voice note, the file goes where you choose to send it. I don't receive any of it.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising and no ad networks
  • No analytics, tracking or cookies for tracking
  • No selling or sharing of data, because I don't have any
  • Fonts and all code are served with the app itself, with no outside fonts or scripts

Hosting

The app files are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, the host may log technical details such as your IP address when your device downloads the app files. That is handled under GitHub's own privacy statement, and I don't receive or use it.

Links to other sites

The app has links to my Instagram, my YouTube channel and a support page. If you open one, you leave the app and that site's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it doesn't collect personal information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update the date at the top of this page.
