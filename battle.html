How this works

Built for live battles, practice reps, and judge panels. Pick a round length, choose your buzzer, hit start — the ring counts down and the buzzer fires the instant time's up.

  • Round length: 60s or 120s for a standard exchange, 150s or 200s for an extended or final round.
  • Buzzer: Voice yells "Time!!", Horn blasts a synthesized air horn, Ringing plays a bell-style alarm. All work offline, no files needed.
  • Tap the ring anytime to pause or resume mid-round.
  • The ring shifts violet → pink → red as the clock runs down, and pulses in the final 10 seconds.
  • Your screen stays awake while a round is running, where your browser supports it — keep the volume up so the room hears the buzzer.
